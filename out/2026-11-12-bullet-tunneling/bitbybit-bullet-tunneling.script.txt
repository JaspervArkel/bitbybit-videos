This bullet just went through a wall.
The wall was fine.
Games don't move objects smoothly.
They jump them forward once per frame, then check for collisions.
A slow object always lands touching the wall.
But a fast bullet can be in front of the wall
on one frame and already behind it on the next.
The check never sees it touching. It's called tunneling.
The fix: sweep the bullet's whole path between frames
and find where it would hit.
Engines call it continuous collision detection.
